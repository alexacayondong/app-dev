#Favorite series/ movies
1. Beauty and the Beast 
   -  Beauty and the Beast is a live-action adaptation of Disney's classic animated film in 2017, featuring Emma Watson as Belle and Dan Stevens as the Beast. This movie tells the story of a young woman who befriends a cursed prince trapped in a beast's form, showing love, kindness, and inner beauty. 
2. Howl's Moving Castle
   - Is a 2004 animated fantasy film directed by Hayao Miyazaki, based on the novel by Diana Wynne Jones. The story follows Sophie, a young woman who is transformed into an old woman because of a witch’s curse, and her adventures with the wizard Howl in his magical moving castle. The movie shows love, self-discovery, and the impact of war, and is renowned for its beautiful animation, imaginative world, and heartfelt storytelling.
3. Wednesday
   -  Is a TV series on Netflix that started in 2022. It is about Wednesday Addams, a dark and spooky girl from The Addams Family. In the series, she goes to a school called Nevermore Academy. She tries to learn about her special powers and solve a spooky mystery. The show is full of dark humor, mystery, and magic. It also explores her journey of self-discovery, her interactions with classmates, and her efforts to uncover secrets about her family and her own powers.
